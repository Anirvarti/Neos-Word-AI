🔬 How the AI works
Raw textStart with example sentences.
TokenizeTurn sentences into words: [I] [want] [to] [learn]
Count patternsRecord which words commonly follow one or two previous words.
ProbabilityIf “Python” appears 35 times after a context out of 100 observations, its estimated next-word probability is 35%.
PredictShow the highest-probability candidates and let the learner choose.
CreateUse words and themes as inputs to a controlled quote generator.
Parent takeaway: the project makes the invisible process visible: words → patterns → probabilities → prediction → creation.
